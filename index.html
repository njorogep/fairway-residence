The Homestay

Run by two hosts, on their own working farm

The Fairway Residence sits on a family smallholding outside Nyeri, where vegetables, fruit and herbs served in the restaurant are grown a short walk from the dining room. Guests describe it less as a hotel and more as being hosted — owners who show you around the farm, staff who remember how you take your coffee.

Rooms open onto a garden with mountain views, and the day tends to slow down here: breakfast on the rooftop, an afternoon walk toward Aberdare, dinner by the outdoor fireplace.

Camping Experience

Relax. Unwind. Escape.

For guests who'd rather sleep under canvas than under a roof, we pitch dome tents right on the garden lawn — the same mountain air and farm breakfast, just a different way to spend the night.

Tents are set up and ready before you arrive, with the main house's bathrooms, WiFi and dining room all just steps away — a good fit for groups, families, or anyone adding an outdoor night onto a room stay.
